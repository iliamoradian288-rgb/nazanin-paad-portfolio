Cookie policy

Last updated: October 2026.

1. What this site stores

Only technical storage strictly needed for the page to work. No analytics, no advertising, no profiling, no external cookies.

NAMEPURPOSEDURATION
Language choiceRemembers your language choice (EN/ES).Persistent, until you clear site data.
Notice dismissalRemembers that you dismissed the cookie notice.Persistent, until you clear site data.

Both live in your browser's local storage (not cookies sent to any server), and nothing leaves your device. Fonts, photos and files are served from this same site, so loading a page makes no external requests.

2. Consent

Technical storage needed for a service you explicitly request (like remembering a language) is exempt from consent under EU and Spanish rules, but you still have the right to this information. That is what this page and the notice are for.

3. How to delete them

Clear this site's data in your browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge all offer Clear browsing data / Website data). The site keeps working; it will simply forget your language choice.

4. Outbound links

Links to Instagram and WhatsApp leave this site and fall under those companies' own cookie and privacy policies.
